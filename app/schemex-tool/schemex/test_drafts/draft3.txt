City Council Approves New Bike Lane Network

The city council voted 6-2 on Tuesday to approve a $4.2 million plan to add 18 miles of protected bike lanes across downtown, with construction set to begin in March.

The plan, first proposed last fall, will connect five existing bike paths into a continuous network stretching from the riverfront district to the university campus. Council member Lena Ortiz, who sponsored the measure, said the project responds to a 40 percent increase in cyclist injuries reported over the past three years.

The funding will come from a combination of state transportation grants and the city's general infrastructure budget, with construction expected to take 14 months. City planners say the new lanes will reduce car lane capacity on three major downtown streets.
